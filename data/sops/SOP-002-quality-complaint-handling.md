# SOP-002 Product Quality Complaint Handling (fictional training document)

Purpose: describe how intake operators record a product quality complaint, such as damaged packaging, a broken seal, a broken tablet, leaking containers or discoloration.

Mandatory fields: product name, batch/lot number in the format LOT-12345, date received, reporter type and a detailed description of the quality issue (appearance, packaging or labelling).

Always capture the batch/lot number exactly as printed. A complaint without a valid batch/lot number cannot be saved. Where several complaints share the same lot number, reviewers compare them to look for patterns; the intake operator only records them.

Physical samples, if returned, are logged by the quality laboratory outside this tool. The tracker records only the reference and status.

Quality complaints follow the same status lifecycle as adverse event reports: New, In Review, then Closed.
