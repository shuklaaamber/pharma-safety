# SOP-005 Terminology Aids for Intake Operators (fictional training document)

This list helps operators and reviewers match reporter wording to standard terms when searching historical reports. It is a vocabulary aid only and carries no clinical meaning.

- dyspnea: also described as difficulty breathing, shortness of breath or breathlessness.
- urticaria: also described as hives, welts or an itchy rash.
- anaphylaxis: also described as a severe allergic reaction.
- nausea and vomiting: also described as feeling queasy or throwing up.
- headache: also described as head pain or migraine.

Quality terms: a torn blister, damaged packaging, a broken seal or a cracked bottle are all packaging defects. Discolouration and color change describe the same quality observation. A chipped tablet or crumbled tablet is a tablet defect.

Search tip: batch and lot numbers such as LOT-88392 should be searched exactly as written.
